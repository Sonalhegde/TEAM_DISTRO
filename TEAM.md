## Problem Statement

Design, build, commission, and validate an automated small-scale fluid handling platform capable of:
1. **Case 1 (Closed-Loop Recirculation and Level Protection):**
   - Maintain closed-loop recirculation at a target flow rate of 3.0 L/min (50 mL/s).
   - Mitigate sump overflow automatically when external liquid is added.
2. **Case 2 (Line Flushing and Safe Shutdown):**
   - Isolate the primary process tank safely using a solenoid valve.
   - Flush the piping network using a dedicated 4.2 L flush reservoir with all three pumps running simultaneously.
   - Transition automatically to a secure OFF state upon flush volume exhaustion.
