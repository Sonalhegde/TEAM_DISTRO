Case 1 · Closed-loop recirculation & level protection

Recirculation Flow: Process fluid leaves the 10 L tank (T-01) through the open solenoid valve (OV-01). Pump-01 runs at 50% PWM and feeds the 4.5 L sump tank. Pump-02 runs at 100% PWM, drawing fluid from the sump and returning it through the FT-01 turbine flow transmitter to T-01 at target 3.0 L/min (50 mL/s).

Sump Overflow Protection: If external fluid enters the sump, the float switch (LS-01) detects high liquid level. Pump-01 stops immediately (0% PWM) while Pump-02 continues pumping at 100% PWM to drain the surcharge back to T-01. Once level clears, Pump-01 automatically restarts.

Case 2 · Line flushing & automatic shutdown

Process Isolation: The solenoid valve OV-01 energizes closed to isolate the 10 L process tank.

Simultaneous 3-Pump Purge: The operator opens manual ball valve BV-01. All three pumps (Pump-01, Pump-02, and Pump-03) run simultaneously to flush clean fluid through the entire piping network.

Automated Safe Shutdown: When the 4.2 L flush volume is depleted and FT-01 senses zero flow, the controller stops all pumps and transitions safely to OFF.
